Sign in at [app.coppertide.ai](https://app.coppertide.ai/). Every signed-in user lands in Shakedown at `/sd/`.

## Ways to sign in

| Method | Who |
|---|---|
| Google | Anyone. Signing in the first time creates the account. |
| GitHub | Anyone. Signing in the first time creates the account. |
| Email and password | By invite only |

A verified email from Google or GitHub matching an existing account signs in to the existing account and links the new sign-in method.

## Your org and its projects

Your account comes with a personal org. An org holds one or more projects, each with its own worlds, scenarios, setups and experiments, edited in the app. Hosted projects live in the database, not in a folder, so hosted Shakedown does not offer **Track with git**. Records of one org stay apart from every other org's: every route asks the authorization layer, and every query in the database runs under row-level security for your org.

The project menu at the top of the sidebar shows the current project. Pick another project to switch: Shakedown opens its **Experiments**. Click **New project** in the same menu, give the project a name and click **Create project** to start with an empty project. You can switch back at any time.

A new project has no worlds, so **Home** offers **Start from an example**. See the [Quickstart](/quickstart).

The account menu at the top right lists your orgs, your personal org first and the current one marked. Pick an org to switch. **Sign out** is at the bottom of the menu.

Pages for inviting people to an org and managing members are not in the app yet.

## Hosted and local

The open-source engine has no accounts and no orgs: one person at one machine. Project files keep the same format in both. See [Self-host](/self-host).
