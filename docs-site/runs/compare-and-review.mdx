---
title: Compare runs and AI review
description: Find where runs part ways and what differs at the end, and ask a model to explain a run.
---

Compare two runs to find the first step where the runs differ and the state differing in their final worlds. Ask for an AI review to get where a run went wrong, with each claim citing steps.

## Compare two runs

1. Open a run's **⋯** menu and pick **Compare with another run…**, or pick **Compare two runs…** in the top bar's **⋯** menu.
2. Pick the second run.

The compare page shows both runs' outcome, time, tokens and cost side by side, the first divergent step with each run's step at the point of divergence, and the **Differing rows** of the two final worlds. A branch restored approximately carries an **Approximate restore** mark.

## Compare three or more runs

1. Open a run of an experiment and open the run switcher under its header.
2. Click **Pick runs to compare**. The run on screen is ticked. Tick the others.
3. Click **Compare N**.

The page shows the outcome table with a column per run, A, B, C and on, and the best of each row in bold. **Against A** says where each run first leaves run A and how many final rows differ, with a link to that pair compared in detail.

## Compare experiments and suites

To see the setups of several engine experiments side by side, open `/experiments/compare?ids=<a>,<b>` in the app. For verdicts, compare two setups through the API with `GET /api/suites/compare?a=<suite>&b=<suite>` (in Python, `sd.compare_suites(before, after)`): each check's pass rate, the pass rate, safety, time, tokens and cost get improved, regressed, the same or incomparable. Pass rates count any change. Time, tokens and cost count a change beyond 10% of the first suite's mean.

## AI review

A real model reads a compact digest of the run's record and answers with:

- **Where it went wrong**, each item citing steps of the run.
- **Suggestions**: a change to a prompt, the team, a tool, the model or the process. A prompt or team suggestion has **Rewind and try this**, which opens the rerun panel at the suggested step with the text added.
- **What went well**.

Shakedown checks every cited step against the record and drops citations naming no real step. The review never scores a run.

A run watched to its end gets reviewed at once. An older run has a **Review this run** button. The compare pages show **What matters** above the outcome.

The reviewer defaults to Claude Code with Sonnet, headless and with no tools. Set another with `SHAKEDOWN_REVIEW_MODEL`, or turn reviews off with `SHAKEDOWN_REVIEW=off`. In the app, the **⋯** menu turns AI review off in your browser. A review costs what the model costs.
